arCade - how to play
========================

Windows: unzip the folder anywhere (not inside a protected folder), double-click arCade.exe.
Linux:   tar xzf arCade-*.tar.gz && cd arCade-* && ./arCade
         (needs the usual desktop libraries: OpenGL and X11, present on any normal desktop install)

Controls are shown on the menu for the selected game.
  Menu:   Up/Down or mouse: choose   Enter or click: play   L: leaderboard   N: change name   Esc: quit
  In a game: Esc returns to the menu.
  Menu background: left mouse paints sand, right mouse paints water (on the right half of the screen).

Scores are saved in scores.txt next to the program. Delete the file to reset the leaderboard.

Command line (optional):
  arCade --version        print the version
  arCade --list           list the games
  arCade --help           all options

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for an unsigned program: choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".
