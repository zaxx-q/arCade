# AGENTS.md - Synthwave City (`synthwave_city`)

Scope: this folder only. Repo rules: `../../AGENTS.md`. Game contract: `../AGENTS.md`. Design brief: `GAME.md`. Owner: Leader

## Overview
Synthwave City: a C99 port of the procedural neon megacity of `highsierralabs/Synthwave_City` (MIT). Fly freely, glide between six viewpoints, or let the tour drone fly (the camera banks into turns, its speed follows its height, and a flight or a tour can be taken over at any moment); score = whole kilometres flown. The game never ends by itself: leaving with Esc ends the run and records the score like any other game (under one kilometre records nothing). Rules and status: `GAME.md`.

## Key Files
- `swc_core.h`, `swc_city.[ch]`, `swc_traffic.[ch]`, `swc_song.[ch]`: pure generators (city plan, traffic, composition); bit-exact with the TypeScript original
- `synthwave_city_logic.[ch]`: pure rules (no raylib): the control mapping (`map_controls`), viewpoints and their glides, new city (`N`), clocks; `init/step/is_over/score`
- `swc_flight.[ch]`: pure flight physics (top speed by height, exponential response, sub-stepped movement with collision, `swc_flight_step_towards` for the glides); moved out of the logic file at M11
- `swc_tour.[ch]`: pure port of the web version's `camera/tour.ts`: the tour is a function of (city, start, seed, time); segments are generated into a fixed buffer, the position is that polyline convolved with a triangle kernel (`SWC_TOUR_SMOOTH` 2.6 s), corner speeds are limited so the rounded corner stays in the street, roll is `0.24 * tanh(lateral / 11)`. Checked against TypeScript goldens at 105 sample times (`tests/test_swc_tour.c`)
- `swc_rig.[ch]`: pure port of `camera/rig.ts`: the phases tour, free, join, blend and ease (`SwcPhase`), the free-flight bank (`free_roll`), the speed-dependent field-of-view kick `7 * smoothstep(90, 420, speed)`, the tour's free look and zoom lens, and the eased viewpoint glides. Checked against a 5700-frame TypeScript golden (`tests/test_swc_rig.c`; worst error 1.8e-12 m and 1.2e-15 rad)
- `synthwave_city_front.h`: the small interface between the front-end files (no raylib types)
- `swc_pack.[ch]`, `swc_shapes.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU buildings layer: instance attributes per box and roof light (same numbers as the web version's `pack.ts`), and the unit box and cylinder
- `swc_ground.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU ground layer: where the street lamps stand (and their colours), the disc the street shader is drawn on, and the one lamp-post mesh; same numbers as the web version's `layers/ground/layout.ts`
- `swc_beams.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU beams layer: which sites are lit, and the seven attributes per beam (searchlights, pillars, laser fans); same numbers as the web version's beams `pack.ts`, and the CPU mirror of the direction function in the shader
- `swc_air.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU air traffic layer: the craft of every sky lane (16 floats each, sorted by shape), the routes of the six hero ships, the hull meshes (lofts of rings), the light tables, and the CPU mirror of the motion in the vertex shaders; same numbers as the web version's `layers/airtraffic/pack.ts` and `shapes.ts`. The layer's seed is the city seed mixed with the layer name (`swc_air_layer_seed`), as the web engine does.
- `swc_street.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU street life layer: the lane table, the CPU mirror of the shader's hash, the field of near cars and the crowd field (nearest first), the vehicle classes (car, van, bus, motorcycle), the alley table (found from the street-level boxes of each block), the block field and walker (people in alleys and on plazas), the vehicle mesh with its three shapes per vertex (car, van, motorcycle); same numbers as the web version's `layers/streetlife/`
- `swc_holo.[ch]`: pure data of the GPU holograms layer: the atlas layout (28 cells in 2048 x 2048), the ad panels (21 floats each), the ticker rings, the wire solids (about 103 thousand vertices); same numbers as the web version's `layers/holograms/` (`layout.ts`, `pack.ts`, `wire.ts`)
- `swc_raster.[ch]`, `swc_font.[ch]`, `swc_atlas.[ch]`: the ad atlas drawn by code at start-up (about 0.14 s): a small software 2D rasteriser with a canvas-like model (paths, fills, strokes, linear gradients with several stops, radial gradients, clips, transforms, and `swc_cv_shift_slice` for the ads' glitch slices), a stroke-built sans face (the letters are this port's own, not the web version's system font; capitals, digits and the four lower-case letters e, l, t, v that the VELVET ad uses), and the 16 wide ads (the four radio-station ads OBSIDIAN, VELVET, STARPORT and PLAZA included), 8 tall ads and 4 ticker strips ported from `atlas.ts`. Cells are independent of drawing order, so a `SwcAtlasJob` can draw them a few per frame, and the half-float texture rows are converted in chunks. No image or font file is read.
- `swc_layers.[ch]`: pure states of the layer switch (the `L` key cycles everything, beams only, air only, street only, holograms only, nothing: six states) and their HUD labels
- `swc_quality.[ch]`: pure quality tiers and the adaptive render scale: the web version's `quality.ts` table (low, medium, high: pixel ratio, MSAA, mirror scale, density, far fade, crowds, scale bounds), the `V` key's order (high, medium, low), render and mirror sizes, crowd and street handover per tier, and a port of `adaptive.ts`. Every quality-dependent number in the GPU layers comes from here (`*_sync(city, q)`), never from a literal.
- `swc_look.[ch]`: pure look of the GPU renderer: the three moods in linear light, their easing, the neon palette, view and projection matrices (roll- and fov-aware; the mirror camera of the reflection pass is built the way three.js does, `swc_mirror_view_matrix`)
- `synthwave_city_render_game.c`: the CPU renderer (rlgl immediate mode, no shaders); also supplies the city geometry to the GPU frame until the GPU layers replace it. It is the fallback and what headless smoke runs use.
- `synthwave_city_render_gpu_game.c`, `swc_gpu.h`, `swc_gpu_core_game.c`, `swc_gpu_sky_game.c`, `swc_gpu_buildings_game.c`, `swc_gpu_beams_game.c`, `swc_gpu_ground_game.c`, `swc_gpu_air_game.c`, `swc_gpu_street_game.c`, `swc_gpu_holo_game.c`, `swc_gpu_post_game.c`: the GPU renderer (GLSL 3.30 through rlgl): HDR target, sky, instanced buildings and their lights, instanced beam ribbons and flares, the ground (street shader on a disc, instanced lamp posts, lamp glows) with the half-size mirror pass the wet streets read, the air traffic (instanced craft hulls, engine lights and trails, hero ships and their lights; also mirrored), street life (a grid of cars worked out in the vertex shader from a lane table, the sidewalk crowd, the alley and plaza crowd read from the alley texture, lamps and light on the road), holograms (instanced ad panels, wire solids, ticker rings, all additive, read the atlas as a linear half-float texture; mirrored), 7-level bloom, final composite; the high tier draws into a 4x multisampled target (raw GL through `rlGetProcAddress`, since rlgl has no API for it; it falls back to no MSAA) that is resolved into the HDR target before the bloom
- `shaders/*`: GLSL sources; `embed_shaders.cmake` turns them into `<build>/swc_shaders.c` at configure time (byte arrays, so no C99 string-length warning); `swc_shaders.h` is its header
- `synthwave_city_game.c`: loop, HUD (with the `H`/`P` hide-UI switch), soundtrack, scripted smoke input, renderer choice (`G` key), layer switch (`L` key), quality tier (`V` key), HUD that scales with the window height, default city seed, headless-only debug switches, `game_step()`/`game_draw()` helpers (shared by `play()` and the guest session), the `swc_session_*` guest API, `game_synthwave_city_def`
- `synthwave_city_session.h`: the guest session API (no raylib types): `swc_session_begin/update/draw/end`, `set_control`, audio pause/mute. The hub runs the city behind its menu through this header (`app/backdrop.c`).
- `tests/test_swc_city.c`, `test_swc_traffic.c`, `test_swc_song.c`, `test_swc_look.c`, `test_swc_pack.c`, `test_swc_shapes.c`, `test_swc_beams.c`, `test_swc_ground.c`, `test_swc_air.c`, `test_swc_street.c`, `test_swc_block.c`, `test_swc_holo.c`, `test_swc_raster.c`, `test_swc_font.c`, `test_swc_atlas.c`, `test_swc_layers.c`, `test_swc_quality.c`, `test_swc_tour.c`, `test_swc_rig.c`, `test_synthwave_city.c`: unit tests (logic only)

## Setup & environment
`cmake --preset logic` needs no raylib. To play: `cmake --preset dev && cmake --build --preset dev && build/dev/bin/arCade`.

## Build & tests
- `cmake --build --preset logic && ctest --preset logic -R "^synthwave_city\."`
- Smoke run (headless build, CPU rasteriser, about 20 s for 300 frames): `build/headless/bin/arCade --smoke synthwave_city --frames 300 --out smoke-out`. The software rasteriser reports `RL_OPENGL_SOFTWARE`, so this always takes the CPU path; its image must stay unchanged by GPU work (only the HUD help text may change, when a key is added).
- GPU frames cannot come from CI. Check them locally by building raylib against a real GL 3.3 context (Mesa llvmpipe is enough) and running the same `--smoke` with the debug switches below, then compare with a render of the web version at seed 1984, clock frozen at 12, one layer at a time (SSIM and MAE over rows 40 to 535, outside the HUD).
- Debug switches, read only when `ctx->headless`: `SWC_RENDERER=cpu`, `SWC_VIEW=1..6`, `SWC_MOOD=0..2`, `SWC_FREEZE=<seconds>`, `SWC_LAYERS=<bit 1 sky, bit 2 legacy city, bit 4 GPU buildings, bit 8 GPU beams, bit 16 GPU ground, bit 32 GPU air traffic, bit 64 GPU street life, bit 128 GPU holograms; default 255>`, `SWC_POST=0`, `SWC_BLOOM=0`, `SWC_REFLECT=0`, `SWC_LIVE=0..15` (the layer switch of the `L` key: 1 beams, 2 air traffic, 4 street life, 8 holograms; works on both renderers), `SWC_POSE=x,y,z,yaw,pitch[,roll[,fov]]` (hold that pose instead of a viewpoint; roll in radians, fov in degrees), `SWC_KEYS=frame:char,...` (type those keys at those frames: `n`, `h`, `t`...), `SWC_TIER=low|medium|high` (the tier the `V` key would be on; default high), `SWC_MSAA=0` (no multisampling on the high tier), `SWC_INTERACTIVE=1` (behave like a real window: adaptive render scale and the atlas spread over frames; off by default so smoke images stay comparable). Never read them in a real window.
- The `L` key is a real feature, not a switch for the harness: `swc_live_layers_set()` (in `synthwave_city_render_game.c`) holds the mask and both renderers leave out what is cleared, whoever would have drawn it (the GPU pass through its `show_*` checks, the CPU renderer through its `g_no_*` flags). A layer added later joins it by adding a bit to `swc_layers.h`.
- The `V` key (was `Q` before M13: `Q` is now down) is real too (GPU renderer only): `swc_tier_next()` gives the order, `swc_gpu_set_tier()` applies it at the next frame, and a new tier starts at its own best render scale. In a real window the city seed is `SWC_DEFAULT_SEED` (1984, as in the web version); headless runs keep the seed of the context, so the CPU smoke image does not change.
- Per-tier check (needs a local GL host, not CI): render the same six viewpoints with `SWC_TIER=low`, `medium` and `high` (with and without `SWC_MSAA=0`) and compare each with the web version at `?q=<tier>` the same way as above. Measured at M8: high with MSAA SSIM 0.988, medium 0.987, low 0.987 (mean over six viewpoints).

## Code style & conventions
- `synthwave_city_State` is about 2.5 MB: keep it in static storage, never on the stack.
- Parity with the web version is the point of the generators: draw RNG values into locals in the original's order, use `double` for generation and `float` only where the original uses `Math.fround`, use stable insertion sorts, and keep `-ffp-contract=off`. Any change to `swc_city.c`, `swc_traffic.c` or `swc_song.c` must keep `test_matches_the_web_version` passing (or update the reference numbers with a reason).
- The city, traffic and song are pure functions of the seed and time; the only state that moves is in `synthwave_city_State`.
- Pure files (everything except `*_game.c`) never include raylib, rlgl, `arcade/gfx.h` or call `rand/srand/time/clock`.
- The CPU renderer is camera-relative and uses only rlgl immediate mode (no shaders): solid pass first, then an additive pass with the depth mask off. Keep the clip planes set and restored around `BeginMode3D`. Leave its output alone: it is the fallback and the smoke image. Since M11 it leans with the camera roll (the sky is drawn upright and the picture turned about its centre), but at roll 0 it hands raylib the world up and so produces exactly the pixels of M10 (checked: six viewpoints, scene rows bit-identical; only the HUD hint line changed).
- Quality tiers: a layer reads density, far fade, crowds and the street handover from `SwcQuality`; the tests in `test_swc_quality.c` hold the packs at the tier densities to the web version's numbers, so change the table only together with the reference numbers. The adaptive render scale runs only when `swc_gpu_set_interactive(true)` (a real window); headless runs keep the top of the tier's range.
- People between the buildings: `swc_street_block_walker_at()` is the CPU mirror of `shaders/sl_block.vert` and `swc_street_build_alleys()` of the web version's `buildAlleyTable`; change any of the three only together with the other two and the golden numbers in `test_swc_block.c`. The alley finder works in fixed scratch (`SPAN_CAP` street-level boxes per cell; the plan puts 16 at most there, and the test fails long before the cap).
- The GPU renderer draws in linear light into an RGBA16F target and does tone, grade and sRGB only in the last pass (`post.frag`), as the web version does. Shader text lives in `shaders/`; a new file there is picked up at the next CMake configure. Only `*_game.c` files include raylib or rlgl; `swc_gpu.h` and `swc_look.h` stay plain C. A GPU failure (shader compile, incomplete framebuffer) must fall back to the CPU renderer, never abort.
- Shaders taken from the web version need no per-file attribution header: the MIT notice in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` covers them. The layer-by-layer match against the web version is the acceptance test of every GPU layer, so keep the reference method above working.
- Camera and input (M11, M13): the controls reach the rig only through `map_controls()`. `ArcadeInput` has `move_z` (E up, Q down) and `shift` since M13 (the owner approved the core change; the monkey leaves both at zero, so no other game's random stream changed): forward is `-move_y`, right is `move_x`, up is `move_z`, boost is Shift or the right mouse button, the wheel zooms the tour lens (tour) or scales the cruise speed (free flight, an extra of this port), a double click takes over from the tour, Space toggles the tour, `1`..`6` glide to a viewpoint, `T` changes the mood, `H` or `P` hides the HUD, `N` builds a new city (seed rules of `seed.ts`: rolled seeds 1..999999, never the current one; the GPU layers resync by `city->seed`) and `V` changes the quality tier. Arrow-key turning of the web version is not ported (the arrows still fly like WASD). The keys come through `in->typed`; never read the keyboard directly.
- Mouse grab (M13), as pointer lock in the web version: nothing is grabbed at the start. A click in free flight, or the double click that takes over from the tour, raises `grab_wanted` in the logic; the front-end (`mouse_grab`) then calls `DisableCursor()` (never in a smoke run, and only while the window has focus) and `swc_set_pointer_lock(s, true)`; while grabbed, moving the mouse in free flight looks around without a button, and on the tour a drag still needs the button. ESC frees the mouse first (`loop_running()` swallows that ESC; the next one leaves the game), losing focus frees it, and leaving the game calls `EnableCursor()`. `swc_set_pointer_lock()` also drops the next frame's pointer delta, because the cursor jumps to the window centre on a grab; keep that if you touch `map_controls()`. The logic never calls raylib: only `pointer_locked` and `grab_wanted` cross the line. The real grab cannot be exercised by CI, so check it by hand in a window after changing it.
- Any change to `swc_tour.c` or `swc_rig.c` must keep `test_swc_tour.c` and `test_swc_rig.c` passing; their golden numbers come from the TypeScript original, so regenerate them from there with a reason, never to make a test pass.
- Smoke runs use `scripted_input()` (the headless monkey would stick to walls); keep it touching every control. Since M11 the film also presses `H` (hide UI, frames 20 to 24), and the tour and glides differ from M10, so the 300-frame CPU image changed once (new camera, new HUD hint line); re-baseline it only for a reason like that.
- Keep `GAME.md` in sync when mechanics change (the report is written from it).
- Follow the game contract in `../AGENTS.md` (loop pattern, determinism, fixed arrays, `finished` semantics).

## Security & safety
- Only write under `ctx->data_dir`; read assets from `ctx->assets_dir`; validate any file you parse. This game reads and writes no files; the soundtrack is rendered into memory with `synth_render`.
- Code and text of the original web app (algorithms, GLSL shader source, hologram ad names) may be used under its MIT licence with the notice in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. No images, fonts or audio files from it may be added; all drawing is generated by code and all audio is synthesised.
